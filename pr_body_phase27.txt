## Candidates Evaluated

1. **Primary Candidate — Iranian Legal Entity National ID (*شناسه ملی اشخاص حقوقی*):**
   - **Authority:** State Organization for Registration of Deeds and Properties (*سازمان ثبت اسناد و املاک کشور* — SSAA) / National Portal for Legal Entities Information (`ilenc.ssaa.ir`).
   - **Regulatory Basis:** Cabinet Decree No. 16145/T42656H (1387/06/10) on National Legal Entity IDs.
   - **Structure:** 11 numeric digits (4-digit jurisdiction code + 6-digit sequence + 1 check digit).
   - **Checksum Evidence & Disagreement Analysis:** Resolved conflicting public descriptions. Confirmed that the consensus prime-weights algorithm with 10th-digit offset (`add = d[9] + 2`, coefficients `[29, 27, 23, 19, 17, 29, 27, 23, 19, 17]`, `rem = total % 11`, `check = 0 if rem == 10 else rem`) matched all records in the manually checked public sample (100%, n=6). Concrete real identifiers are intentionally omitted from committed artifacts.
   - **Decision:** **CONDITIONAL GO** | **Recommended Integration:** **OPT-IN DETECTOR** (`IR_LEGAL_ENTITY_ID`).
   - **Condition:** No primary statutory publication of checksum arithmetic found; implementation relies on strong multi-language technical consensus and empirical verification; must remain strictly opt-in.

2. **Secondary Candidate — Iranian Postal Code (*کد پستی ده رقمی*):**
   - **Authority:** National Post Company of the Islamic Republic of Iran (*شرکت ملی پست*).
   - **Structure:** 10 numeric digits encoding geographical sector and building unit.
   - **Checksum:** No mathematical checksum specification was identified in the authoritative Iranian 10-digit postal code standard.
   - **Offline Limitation:** Format validation alone cannot verify validity without online database lookup. A generic 10-digit numeric detector would cause severe false-positive collisions.
   - **Decision:** **HOLD** | **Recommended Integration:** **PATTERN RULE** only.

3. **Secondary Candidate — Company Registration Number (*شماره ثبت شرکت‌ها*):**
   - **Authority:** General Directorate for Registration of Companies and Non-Commercial Institutions.
   - **Structure:** Variable length (1–6 digits), scoped locally to individual registry offices (not nationally unique without city/county context).
   - **Checksum:** No mathematical checksum specification was identified in reviewed materials.
   - **Decision:** **NO-GO** for standalone detector | **Recommended Integration:** **PATTERN RULE** only.

4. **Secondary Candidate — Economic / Tax Identifier (*کد اقتصادی / شماره اقتصادی*):**
   - **Authority:** Iranian National Tax Administration (INTA / *سازمان امور مالیاتی*).
   - **Ecosystem:** Under INTA circular No. 200/1401/30, corporate taxpayers use their 11-digit Legal Entity ID as their economic number. Natural persons use 14-digit numbers, and electronic invoices use 22-character unique identifiers.
   - **Decision:** **HOLD** | **Recommended Integration:** **RESEARCH ONLY / OUT OF SCOPE**.
